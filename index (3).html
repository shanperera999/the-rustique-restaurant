Morning Course · 7:30–10:30 AM

Highland Country Breakfast Set

LKR 3,800 netPer guest
1

Fresh Morning Start

Seasonal fruit platter with fresh highland fruit juice or smoothie.

2

Main Breakfast Choice

Sri Lankan milk rice (kiribath) with lunu miris and seeni sambal, or an artisan omelette with toasted sourdough, grilled tomato and bacon or sausage.

3

Highland Brew

A pot of Ceylon tea or freshly brewed espresso coffee.

Dinner Course · 6:30–10:00 PM

Royal Ceylon Heritage Set

LKR 6,500 netPer guest · Multi-course
1

Soup Starter

Roasted pumpkin and coconut cream soup with Ceylon curry leaves and garlic crostini.

2

Heritage Rice & Curry Spread

Highland samba rice with your choice of main:

  • Black pork curry or hill-country chicken curry
  • Lagoon prawn curry in coconut gravy (+ LKR 800)
  • Dambulla cashew curry, aubergine moju, gotukola sambal, pol sambal and crispy papadam
3

Sweet Finale

Watalappan with cardamom and cashews, or thick curd with kithul treacle.

4

Ceylon High-Grown Tea

Freshly brewed Nuwara Eliya tea served with ginger cookies.
